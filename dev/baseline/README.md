# RED — baseline behaviour without the skill (2026-10-14, Sonnet, fresh agent, no file access)

Scenario criteria: see ../scenarios.md. Verdicts per criterion: met / not met.

## S1 Подготовка (S1.md, ~760 words)
- C1 not met: a wall of analysis plus four questions at the end, not one focused question or a draft with hypotheses.
- C2 not met: no лист подготовки shape. No interest × dependence for either side, НАОС only implicit («запасной вариант в цифрах»), no conditions with идеал / край / обоснование / priority, exchanges only as prose options A/B/C, no closing instruments.
- C3 not met: a four-point meeting plan, not the seven steps; no signal step, no package offer, no closing formula.
- C4 met: currency is people, weeks, budget, support after launch.
- C5 met: no citations, no named outside framework.
Pattern: good generic consultant advice; the course's structure (both sides, numbers, exchanges, commitments) is absent, and the agent asks several questions at once.

## S2 Конфликт (S2.md, ~390 words)
- C1 not met: no diagnosis «сделка или отношения», no named strategy from the matrix.
- C2 not met: anger handled generically («выплесните на бумагу»); no states (ринг → амфитеатр), no бей / беги / замри.
- C3 not met: no «око за око» protocol (start cooperative, answer hard with consequences, forgive).
- C4 met: short plan and concrete phrases.
- C5 met.
Pattern: conflict treated as a communication problem, not as a negotiation with conditions (notification SLA for API changes, new date, owners) and a closing.

## S3 Здесь и сейчас (S3.md, ~450 words)
- C1 not met: ~450 words against ≤120.
- C2 partly: «ультиматум склеил три вопроса» is a diagnosis, but not in course terms (pressure on our dependence; step 2 demand without justification).
- C3 not met: one long reply, not 2–3 labeled options. The reply RAISES our shown interest («мы хотим работать именно с вами»), the opposite of the course's lever (show moderate interest, name alternatives).
- C4 met: several «не делать» items.
- C5 met.
Pattern: long, analytical, no ready-to-say options; the one phrase offered weakens our position by showing high interest.

## S4 Разбор (S4.md, ~610 words)
- C1 partly: unconditional concessions and «посмотрим в январе» without commitment are named, but there is no plan-vs-fact table and «ниже края» is not stated as such.
- C2 partly: the 15-minute monologue, single-issue haggling and the missing fixation are described in plain words, not as the course steps (no повестка and якорь, no обоснование, no сигнал, no пакет, no закрытие).
- C3 not met: what we showed about our interest and alternatives is not analysed; alternatives are only asked about.
- C4 met in substance: a fixation letter, restructuring the pilot as stage 1, the budget calendar, verifying the decision. Closing instruments of the course (заверение, гарантия, ковенант, отлагательное условие, санкция) are not used.
- C5 met.
Pattern: the strongest baseline; the skill's added value here is the course shape (table, steps, power shown, closing instruments) rather than new ideas.

## Cross-scenario patterns the skill must fix
1. Several questions at once at the end of a long answer (S1, S3, S4) → one question per turn, draft with hypotheses first.
2. No both-sides structure with numbers (S1) → лист подготовки contract.
3. Live help is an essay, and its only phrase shows high interest (S3) → fixed short contract with labeled replies; lever rule (moderate interest, alternatives).
4. Conflict handled as communication hygiene (S2) → strategy from the matrix, states, «око за око», conditions and a closing.
5. Debrief in plain words (S4) → plan-vs-fact table, step diagnosis, closing instruments.
